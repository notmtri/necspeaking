About this document

This is the technical companion to the International Faculty Community Platform pilot proposal. It is written for VinUniversity IT, HR and Faculty Affairs, and sets out what will be built, how it connects to VinUniversity’s systems, how it is secured, and what is needed from each team to deliver a pilot by 24 December 2026. Items marked [IT to confirm] are proposals for discussion, not assumptions about VinUniversity’s current systems.

1Summary

The Faculty Hub is a small, secure web application for VinUniversity’s international faculty. It combines two things faculty asked for: a trusted information hub and a buddy programme for newcomers. A working pilot can be ready by 24 December 2026 if IT access and HR funding are agreed in the first week of December.

What it does

  • Information hub: a “Who to contact” directory of university offices; official guides on visas and work permits, tax, banking, housing and healthcare, each owned and reviewed by the responsible office; and dated, faculty-recommended service providers.
  • Buddy programme: newcomers ask for an experienced colleague; a coordinator in Faculty Affairs confirms each match; both receive an introduction and a check-in a few weeks later.

Why these two

In the November 2026 survey of 42 international faculty, the most valued topics were visas and work permits (74%), banking, taxes and transfers (71%), and housing (60%). The most wanted feature was a “Who to contact at VinUniversity” directory (60%), and the hardest thing to resolve was understanding university processes (60%). The buddy programme serves newcomers, who were under-represented in the survey (79% of respondents had been at VinUniversity for more than a year).

How it is built

  • Faculty sign in with their VinUniversity Microsoft account (single sign-on with multi-factor authentication). The Hub stores no passwords.
  • Staff details (name, email, unit, title, employment status) come from VinUniversity’s records, read-only, through a route IT chooses. The Hub never writes to VinUniversity’s HR systems.
  • The Hub’s own data (buddy matches, guides, providers, optional profile details) is kept in its own encrypted database on VinUniversity-controlled infrastructure.

Decisions needed in the first week of December

  1. IT: choose the platform (Section 4), the source of staff data (Section 5) and the hosting, and name a technical contact.
  2. HR: approve funding for the pilot build and running costs (Section 9).
  3. Faculty Affairs: confirm a buddy coordinator and the owner of each official guide.

5Integration with VinUniversity data

The Hub uses VinUniversity’s data as the single source of truth for who someone is and where they work. It reads only the fields it needs, never writes back, and stops showing anyone who leaves.

5.1Identity

Sign-in is handled entirely by Entra ID. The Hub receives a signed token with the user’s identifier, name and email, and checks that they belong to an allowed group (for example, all academic staff). Disabling someone’s VinUniversity account removes their access immediately.

5.2Staff records: three options for IT

OptionHow it worksNotes
1. Entra directory (simplest)The Hub reads profile fields from Microsoft Graph with read-only, admin-approved permissionsWorks if HR already keeps department and job title up to date in Entra ID
2. Read-only HR view or APIIT provides a read-only view or API exposing only the agreed fieldsMost accurate; needs a small piece of work by the HR system’s owner
3. Scheduled exportHR exports the agreed fields once a day to a secure location the Hub readsQuickest to arrange; data up to a day old

In all three, the Hub receives only the fields below, through a service account with read-only rights, and synchronises once a day.

What the Hub does not do

  • It never writes to VinUniversity’s HR or identity systems.
  • It never stores passwords, salary, passport or visa numbers, or home addresses.
  • It never shares data with third parties or external analytics services.

6Security

The Hub holds personal data about colleagues, so security is designed in from the start and checked by IT before launch. Nothing goes live without IT’s sign-off.

AreaControls
Identity and accessVinUniversity single sign-on only, with MFA through Conditional Access; no local accounts or passwords. Role-based permissions checked on the server for every request. Sessions expire after inactivity. Administrator roles reviewed each semester.
Data protectionTLS 1.2 or higher for all traffic, with HSTS. Database and backups encrypted at rest. Data kept on VinUniversity-controlled infrastructure [IT to confirm location requirements]. Minimum fields only (Section 5.3).
Application securityBuilt against the OWASP Top 10: parameterised database queries, output encoding, CSRF protection, strict Content Security Policy, input validation, secure cookie settings, and rate limiting. No third-party code loaded in the browser.
Secure developmentCode review for every change; automated tests; dependency vulnerability scanning; secrets in a key vault; only fictional test data outside production; production access limited to IT.
Logging and monitoringAudit log of every administrative action, content change and role change. Access and error logs sent to IT’s monitoring [IT to confirm]. Alerts on repeated failures.
Testing before launchIT security review of the design and code; vulnerability scan of the staging environment; all critical and high findings fixed before go-live.
ResilienceDaily encrypted backups with a tested restore; separate staging environment for changes.
IncidentsFollows VinUniversity’s incident process [IT to confirm]; any incident affecting personal data reported to IT and Legal immediately.

6.1Privacy and compliance

  • Consent: every optional personal field has its own consent, purpose and date, and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Rights: faculty can see, download and delete their optional data from their profile.
  • Retention: profiles are hidden when someone leaves and their optional data deleted after a period set by policy [Legal to confirm].
  • Legal review: the design aims to meet Vietnam’s Law on Personal Data Protection and Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP, including any required data-processing impact assessment. VinUniversity’s Legal or Data Protection function should confirm this before launch.

What the date depends on

The 24 December date holds only if IT access is in place by 4 December and the offices supply their content by 11 December. Each week of delay to either moves launch by about a week. If hosting cannot be ready in time, option B (Microsoft 365) is the fallback.
