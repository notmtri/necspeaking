# Product

## Register

product

## Users

K12 students preparing for NEC speaking and related English speaking assessments. They use the app during focused self-study sessions to practice prompts, compare sample responses, simulate test conditions, and review feedback without needing a mentor beside them.

## Product Purpose

necs. helps students improve NEC speaking performance through speech analysis, sample speech study, mock simulations, profiles, and community context. Success means a student can start practice quickly, understand the next improvement step, and return over time with less friction and more confidence.

## Brand Personality

Educational, technological, minimal, and professional. The interface should feel credible and calm, with enough warmth for K12 learners but without becoming childish or decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered educational portals, excessive explanatory copy, decorative lines, redundant cards, and unnecessary interface items. Avoid gamified or juvenile visuals that reduce credibility for serious competition preparation.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next practice action obvious.
2. Keep the interface calm enough for study.
3. Prefer concise labels over repeated explanations.
4. Use data and feedback only when it helps the student decide what to do next.
5. Keep professional credibility while remaining approachable for K12 students.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Preserve keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion behavior, readable contrast, and clear labels for students using phones, laptops, and shared school devices.
